# LUMINA™ Brand Guidelines

## Brand Idea

**Radiance, made visible.** LUMINA makes a familiar active feel sensorial and elevated. The brand lives at the meeting point of luminous efficacy and quiet ritual: polished, warm, and never overworked.

## Personality & Voice

| LUMINA is | LUMINA is not |
| --- | --- |
| Assured, sensorial, and precise | Flashy, breathless, or overly precious |
| Editorial and composed | Coldly clinical or science-fictional |
| Benefit-aware | Claim-heavy or medical |
| Luxurious through restraint | Decorative for decoration's sake |

Write in short, complete sentences. Begin with the desired feeling, then ground it in a simple product benefit. Use active verbs: *reveal, brighten, replenish, revitalize*. Prefer “brighter-looking skin” where substantiation is not supplied. Avoid miracle language, urgency tactics, exclamation marks, and stacked superlatives.

## Color System

| Token | Role | Hex | Usage |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `--lumina-amber` | Product signature | `#C66A08` | Product-led highlights; never a full-page field |
| `--lumina-gold` | Metallic accent | `#B7832F` | Rules, icon strokes, premium emphasis |
| `--lumina-ivory` | Primary canvas | `#F8F1E7` | Page backgrounds and spacious content areas |
| `--lumina-stone` | Surface depth | `#D8C4A6` | Secondary surfaces and tonal layering |
| `--lumina-ink` | Primary text | `#302D2A` | Body copy and navigation |
| `--lumina-olive` | Botanical note | `#4A5035` | Image-only accent; use sparingly in UI |

Gold is an accent, not a default text color. Keep long-form copy in Ink on Ivory. Where gold is placed over image, test it for contrast rather than assuming the metallic association is enough.

## Typography

**Display / wordmark:** A high-contrast editorial serif with elegant stress (recommended: Cormorant Garamond or Bodoni Moda). Use uppercase for campaign statements, modest tracking, and optical sizing where supported.

**Text / interface:** A clean sans-serif with open forms (recommended: Manrope or Inter). Use sentence case for body copy, labels, and navigation.

| Role | Desktop guidance | Mobile guidance |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Display headline | 64–88 px / 0.95–1.0 | 42–56 px / 1.0 |
| Section title | 40–56 px / 1.05 | 30–38 px / 1.1 |
| Body | 17–18 px / 1.55 | 16–17 px / 1.55 |
| Eyebrow / benefit row | 12–14 px / uppercase | 11–12 px / uppercase |

Do not condense display type, add outlines, or use more than two type families. The contrast between serif statement and sans-serif utility is the system.

## Image Art Direction

The bottle is a piece of glassware, not a prop. Stage it upright, fully legible, and lit with warm directional daylight. Pair it with creamy marble, soft-focus florals, olive foliage, clear water, and quiet spa objects. Compose with shallow depth of field and clean reflections.

Water should look precise and luminous: a halo, splash, or suspended droplets that frame the bottle without obscuring the label. Avoid clinical labs, icy blue casts, busy bathrooms, hands touching the product, hard flash, or heavily saturated color grading.

## Layout & Hierarchy

1. Establish the hero: product or display headline.
2. Deliver one short supporting line.
3. Offer proof points as a compact benefit row or feature module.
4. Present one primary CTA.

For landscape compositions, use a copy-left/product-right balance with protected negative space behind type. For vertical social, prioritize headline, bottle, brand/product name, benefits, then CTA. Never force both a long headline and a dense feature panel into the same first viewport.

## Spacing & Grid

Use an 8 px base scale: `8 / 16 / 24 / 32 / 48 / 64 / 80 / 96 / 128`. Use 12 columns on desktop, 6 on tablet, and 4 on mobile. Desktop page gutters should be 64–96 px; mobile gutters 20–24 px. Let hero compositions breathe—space is an active part of the luxury signal.

## Components

| Component | Specification |
| --- | --- |
| Primary CTA | Gold fill, white label, 48 px minimum height, 12–16 px horizontal padding, clear hover/focus state. Label: `SHOP NOW ->`. |
| Secondary CTA | Text link or transparent outlined treatment; never competes with primary CTA. |
| Benefit row | Uppercase sans label, small gold dot separators, three items maximum. |
| Feature module | Fine-line gold icon, uppercase title, one restrained sentence, optional 1 px gold divider. |
| Eyebrow | Sans-serif uppercase label used to orient, never to decorate. |
| Product card | Large product image, product name, short benefit, price/CTA only where commerce is in scope. |

## Consistency Rules

- Keep the bottle's amber glass, gold cap, and clear dropper consistent across every asset.
- Use one campaign headline and one CTA per composition; repetition should come from the system, not density.
- Preserve a legible product label and an uncluttered silhouette in all crops.
- Use warm neutrals and natural sunlight; avoid cool-blue lighting and black-on-gold luxury clichés.
- Keep benefit language parallel: **Brightens. Hydrates. Revitalizes.**
- Use the trademark consistently in formal brand references; omit it only in cramped UI where legal guidance permits.
- Ensure essential text, CTA, and product information exist as live accessible content in digital implementations.
