# LUMINA™ Luxury Skincare Campaign

## Overview

LUMINA is a visual identity and campaign system for a premium Vitamin C Serum. The work asks a simple question: how can a product associated with “glow” make that promise visible before a word is read? The answer became an image world built from amber liquid, reflective water, sun-warmed stone, and a bottle treated like an object of desire.

The result is a compact launch system spanning a landing-page hero, paid social, organic social, and lifestyle editorial—each execution distinct in composition, but unmistakably LUMINA.

## The Challenge

Vitamin C imagery often falls into one of two modes: generic clinical proof or bright, high-energy beauty content. Neither could carry the quiet confidence expected of a luxury campaign. The challenge was to create a richer emotional read while keeping the product category and core benefit immediately understandable at the small sizes where social work lives.

## Research

The visual audit focused on the cues that make prestige beauty feel considered: scarcity of elements, controlled light, tactile surfaces, well-resolved product photography, and typography that behaves like editorial design rather than advertising noise. The strategic takeaway was not to add more evidence claims; it was to give familiar benefits a more memorable setting.

## Creative Direction

### Radiance Unleashed

The campaign platform turns radiance into a physical event. Water wraps the bottle in moments of suspended motion. The transparent dropper and amber formula catch the light. Marble provides stillness; botanicals introduce life; gold signals finish and value. Together, those ingredients create a composed contrast: active formula, calm ritual.

The palette stays close to the product itself—amber, gold, ivory, stone, and deep olive—so every touchpoint returns the eye to the bottle.

## AI Workflow

AI-supported image exploration accelerated the search for lighting, material behavior, water movement, and composition. The important design work came after generation: establishing a fixed pack silhouette, defining the permissible environment and palette, selecting a limited number of visual devices, and rejecting images that looked impressive but did not serve the system.

This made the process art-directed rather than prompt-led. Each chosen frame was evaluated for label legibility, negative space for copy, plausible light direction, product scale, and its ability to sit beside the rest of the campaign.

## Design Process

1. **Define the signature.** The amber bottle, gold cap, and clear dropper became non-negotiable identity assets.
2. **Build the world.** Warm daylight, pale marble, water, and botanicals established a repeatable visual vocabulary.
3. **Create the master frame.** A landscape hero set the balance of editorial headline, benefit information, CTA, and product drama.
4. **Adapt by hierarchy, not by cropping.** Square and vertical formats were recomposed around the same sequence: promise, product, benefits, action.
5. **Refine for use.** Copy was reduced to its essential messages, and the component system defined how the campaign can extend into digital product storytelling.

## Campaign Assets

| Asset | Role in the system |
| --- | --- |
| Product Hero | The clearest product proof: bottle, formula color, water energy, and a concise commerce cue. |
| Website Hero Banner | The master brand statement, combining editorial headline, product proposition, feature modules, and CTA. |
| Instagram Feed | A fast-recognition unit designed to stop the scroll without sacrificing the quiet visual tone. |
| Instagram Story | A vertical reveal that uses scale, centered product staging, and a bottom-weighted action area. |
| Facebook Ad | A performance-ready adaptation with immediate category clarity, benefit row, and legible CTA. |
| Lifestyle Editorial | The slowest moment in the campaign: a serene ritual setting that broadens the product from object to lifestyle. |

## Final Outcome

LUMINA now has a coherent visual language with enough structure to scale and enough restraint to remain premium. The campaign does not ask the audience to imagine radiance; it stages it. From the first hero impression to the smallest social frame, the amber bottle remains the recognisable point of return.

## What I Learned

Premium is a discipline of omission. The strongest outcomes came from repeating a small number of highly controlled decisions—one product silhouette, one light quality, one material palette, one typographic contrast—and letting each have room to register. AI imagery became more useful once the visual rules were strict enough to make curation decisive.
