# LUMINA™ Campaign Copywriting Library

This is the approved campaign copy inventory captured from the supplied visual suite. It is organized for reuse across advertising, social, and a future landing page. Preserve the short, composed cadence; do not stack multiple headlines or benefit lists in a single placement.

| Brand | **LUMINA™** |

| Attribution | **Made by LA** / **MADE BY LA** |
| Product lockup | **LUMINA™ Vitamin C Serum** |

## Campaign Headlines

- **REVEAL YOUR NATURAL GLOW.**
- **RADIANCE UNLEASHED.**
- **RADIANCE STARTS HERE.**

Use one headline per placement. Set as display serif in uppercase. The period is part of the campaign's confident, editorial tone.

## Supporting Product Copy

- Brighten, hydrate, and revitalize your skin with our premium Vitamin C Serum.
- Reveal brighter, healthier-looking skin with our premium Vitamin C Serum.
- Brightening & Revitalizing.
- BRIGHTENS & REVITALIZES.

**Preferred digital adaptation:**

> Reveal brighter-looking skin with LUMINA™ Vitamin C Serum.

This wording is clearer, more concise, and avoids implying a medical outcome. Confirm all final product claims against substantiation before publication.

## Calls to Action

- **SHOP NOW ->**
- Shop LUMINA™ Vitamin C Serum

Use **SHOP NOW ->** as the campaign’s primary visual CTA. Use the product-specific sentence form where a screen-reader label, text link, or search-ad destination requires additional clarity.

## Attribution & Badge Copy

- **MADE BY LA**
- **AI MARKETING CAMPAIGN • PORTFOLIO PROJECT**

The portfolio-project badge belongs in presentation or case-study contexts, not consumer-facing campaign placements.

## Usage & Safety Copy

- For external use only. Patch test recommended. Not a medical claim.

Set safety copy in a legible sans-serif, never embedded only in imagery, and route final wording through legal/product review.

## Messaging Hierarchy by Channel

| Channel | Recommended sequence |
| --- | --- |
| Landing-page hero | Headline -> supporting product line -> benefit row -> primary CTA |
| Paid social | Headline or product name -> 1–3 benefits -> CTA |
| Instagram feed | Headline -> product image -> benefit row; CTA in caption or link treatment |
| Instagram Story | Headline -> centered product -> benefit row -> CTA / link sticker |
| Product detail | Product name -> short promise -> feature modules -> ingredient/usage details -> CTA |

## Copy Guardrails

- Say less, with more intent. One claim territory per message is enough.
- Lead with the feeling of radiance; follow with a plain-language product benefit.
- Keep all-caps to headlines, labels, benefit rows, and buttons.
- Avoid “miracle,” “instant results,” “flawless,” “cure,” and unsubstantiated clinical language.
- Do not imply medical treatment, prevention, or guaranteed results.
