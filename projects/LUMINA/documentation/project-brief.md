# LUMINA™ Luxury Skincare Campaign

## Project Overview

LUMINA is a campaign concept for a premium Vitamin C Serum. It frames skincare as a private, light-filled ritual: an amber bottle set against honed marble, soft botanicals, reflective water, and late-morning sun. The visual world is deliberately edited. Every material has a job: amber makes the formula visible, water signals freshness, and gold gives the product its jewelry-like finish.

The campaign system was designed to hold together across a landing-page hero, paid social, organic social, and lifestyle editorial without diluting the core image of the product.

## Campaign Goal

Create desire before detail. The first impression should be *radiance*; the second should be a clear product proposition: a Vitamin C Serum positioned around brighter-looking, hydrated, revitalized-looking skin. From there, guide the viewer to one confident action: **Shop Now**.

## Audience

**Primary:** Design-literate skincare shoppers, 25–45, who see daily skincare as both efficacy and ritual. They buy into texture, packaging, ingredient familiarity, and an elevated visual experience.

**Mindset:** They are not seeking a loud transformation story. They want a product that feels credible, considered, and easy to incorporate into a polished routine.

## Deliverables

- Product hero / product-detail visual
- Website landing-page hero banner
- Instagram feed creative (1:1)
- Instagram Story creative (9:16)
- Facebook ad creative (landscape)
- Lifestyle editorial visual
- Brand, case-study, and copywriting documentation

## Creative Platform: Radiance, Made Visible

The campaign turns an intangible benefit into a tangible image. Light, glass, water, and reflective stone work together to show radiance rather than merely describe it. The product is never incidental: it is composed as the hero object, with the environment acting as atmosphere and proof of care.

## Design Objectives

- Establish the amber bottle and gold cap as the distinctive brand asset.
- Build a recognizable visual signature from warm light, ivory stone, controlled water movement, and botanical depth.
- Use editorial restraint: large fields of calm, minimal copy, and one unmistakable focal point per frame.
- Make each format feel native to its channel while preserving the same brand codes.

## Digital Experience Direction

- Use the landing page as a paced product story: **promise -> proof points -> ingredient/ritual detail -> product CTA**.
- Keep the primary CTA visible in the hero and repeat it only at meaningful decision points.
- On mobile, lead with the product and headline; defer supporting feature modules until below the first viewport.
- Respect the campaign's quiet tone with restrained motion: slow light shifts, subtle water shimmer, and short, purposeful transitions. Support `prefers-reduced-motion`.
- Build imagery with responsive focal-point crops so the bottle, label, and headline are never compromised.

## Technical Objectives

- Meet WCAG AA contrast for live text and controls; do not rely on image-embedded text for essential product or CTA information.
- Keep page performance premium: serve responsive WebP/AVIF assets, lazy-load below-the-fold imagery, and reserve layout space to prevent shift.
- Treat the visual assets as art-directed crops with defined focal areas, not generic background images.
- Use semantic headings, descriptive alt text, visible keyboard focus states, and CTA labels that remain clear out of context.
- Validate all skincare claims with the client/legal team before release; the campaign language should remain cosmetic and non-medical.

## Success Criteria

- LUMINA is identifiable within the first three seconds: brand, Vitamin C Serum category, and radiance proposition are clear.
- The full suite feels authored as one campaign, not resized executions of one layout.
- Product, headline, and CTA maintain hierarchy at desktop, mobile, feed, Story, and ad-viewing sizes.
- The campaign earns a premium response through clarity and restraint, while still providing a direct path to purchase.
